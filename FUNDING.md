# Funding

*Hoard is built by one dev. No investors, no ads, no data selling. Every € goes to keeping it running and improving.*

## Infrastructure costs (per user/month)

| Service | Free tier user | Pro user (100 GB) |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Supabase** (DB + auth + realtime) | ~$0.01 | ~$0.10 |
| **Fly,io** (server hosting) | ~$0.01 | ~$0.05 |
| **Cloudflare R2** (storage) | **$0.03** | **$1.50** |
| **Total/user** | **~$0.05** | **~$1.65** |

> Supabase/Fly.io are estimates — they charge by usage (requests, CPU, bandwidth), not per user. Cloudflare R2 is the only predictable cost.

Pro subscriptions (1.99 €/month) are meant to cover their own costs and **subsidize free tier** users. Right now there are no Pro users, so the free tier runs at a loss.

Stripe fee: ~0.5 € per Pro subscription. A Pro user can consume up to ~$1.65 in infrastructure — the margin is thin by design.

## Monthly baseline (fixed)
- **Domain / SSL / misc**: ~$4.5
- **Server baseline** (idle Fly,io VM): ~$5–10
- **Development** (tooling, CI, dependencies, testing infra): ~$25

**Total fixed: ~$30–45/month**

## What your sponsorship covers
- Server infrastructure (sync, auth, storage)
- Development time (features, fixes, support)
- Keeping the free tier alive with 2GB at the moment.
